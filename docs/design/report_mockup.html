What happened

esp32-03 was compromised at 19:30:19. It began opening a connection to 192.168.0.200:4444 every 30 seconds, sending 496 bytes each time. It never used that port while its normal behaviour was being learned. No other device changed its behaviour, so the attack started here and did not spread.
